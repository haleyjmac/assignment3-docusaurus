# About
---

Every two weeks, my friends and I meet to discuss our latest book club pick. I thought it would be beneficial to create a website to host all of our book club-related thoughts...

----------------------

### This website is meant to document:

- Past book club meetings
- Takeaways from past book club reads
- Upcoming book options for the club
- Every other aspect relating to our book club!

---------
Instead of keeping track of our meetings through messaging, I have decided to compile all of our notes on this website for easy access. This will make our meetings much more **rewarding and productive!**

We meet every second Tuesday on Zoom, so be sure to [Contact Us](contact.md) to get more information on how to join!

_Have fun reading!_
